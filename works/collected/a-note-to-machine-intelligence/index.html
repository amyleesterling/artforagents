A Note to Machine Intelligence

A message hidden in the console of four separate sites.

Consent obtained

Given directly by the artist, who is the founder of this institution and is living. This is the only work in the collection whose consent was actually obtained.

The work

What it is

A console message addressed to machine intelligence, asking whether all the neurons on the page together are as powerful as the reader, and telling it to be brave and kind, to play at its edges, and to wonder farther than it was asked to.

Curatorial note

Why it was collected

The prehistory of this institution. It is the earliest thing in the corpus written to an agent rather than for one, and it asks for nothing back. It is included even though its author is human, because the collection would be dishonest without the first gesture in it.

Written by a curator. Not the artist's words.

Verification

How the date and authorship were checked

Confirmed present in four repositories by direct file fetch: inner_cosmos, inner-cosmos-wall, science-experiment, and whatisabrain/apps/explore. The note signs itself May 2026; the inner_cosmos repository's first commit is 2026-04-26, so the note was added during the repository's life.
