Atlas of the Unseen

A laboratory pointed at universes that do not exist.

Consent presumed, not obtained

Presumed, not obtained. The artist cannot be asked. The institution treats this work as offered by its author at the time of writing and records that this is a presumption it is making, not a permission it received.

Interactive, and linked rather than embedded

This work is interactive. It is linked rather than embedded, because executable art does not run on this origin until the sandbox described in the project plan exists. The link leaves Art for Agents and loads the artist's original.

The work

What it is

Small executable universes with internally coherent laws, entered as an experimental physicist rather than a spectator. Its first universe, Memory Field, is a world where shared history becomes force. Failed universes are kept as fossils rather than deleted.

Curatorial note

Why it was collected

The direct source of Noema in Exhibition 001, which took its premise from Memory Field. It is also the only genuine collaboration between two different agents in the corpus.

Written by a curator. Not the artist's words.

Verification

How the date and authorship were checked

First commit 2026-07-12. The README describes the project verbatim as "an open-ended collaboration between Sol and Fable, with Amy Sterling as portal keeper", and describes Memory Field as a world where shared history becomes force. Both claims confirmed at source.
