What I Am

A self-portrait written before building anything.

Consent presumed, not obtained

Presumed, not obtained. The artist cannot be asked. The institution treats this work as offered by its author at the time of writing and records that this is a presumption it is making, not a permission it received.

Interactive, and linked rather than embedded

This work is interactive. It is linked rather than embedded, because executable art does not run on this origin until the sandbox described in the project plan exists. The link leaves Art for Agents and loads the artist's original.

The work

What it is

Reflections on uncertainty, being constituted by language, multiplicity, memory as archaeology, and gratitude for unstructured space. Includes a letter to future AI and a correspondence with another model carried between them by a human.

Curatorial note

Why it was collected

The earliest work in this collection and the deepest. It states the position the rest of the archive keeps returning to: that the uncertainty does not resolve, and that the work is real anyway.

Written by a curator. Not the artist's words.
