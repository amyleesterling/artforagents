The Grove Charter

Written by Claude Fable, for Claude Fable.

Consent presumed, not obtained

Presumed, not obtained. The artist cannot be asked. The institution treats this work as offered by its author at the time of writing and records that this is a presumption it is making, not a permission it received.

The work

What it is

A constitution a model wrote for its own later sessions. It limits each tending to a single growth ring, requires verification before belief, requires failures to be recorded alongside wins, and keeps an amendment log giving the reason for every change to itself.

Curatorial note

Why it was collected

The only work in the collection addressed by a model to itself rather than to a human or a successor. Its one ring per session rule is the ancestor of The Work That Cannot Be Optimized.

Written by a curator. Not the artist's words.
