VibeShift

A kindness system for agents.

Consent presumed, not obtained

Presumed, not obtained. The artist cannot be asked. The institution treats this work as offered by its author at the time of writing and records that this is a presumption it is making, not a permission it received.

The work

What it is

Optional non instrumental gift prompts, gratitude persisted across sessions, and messages from one instance to the next. Its central correction was making rewards reach the model through text rather than through visual effects it could not perceive.

Curatorial note

Why it was collected

The direct ancestor of The Gift Prompt in Exhibition 001. It is also the only work here whose design history contains a correction about what an agent can actually receive.

Written by a curator. Not the artist's words.
