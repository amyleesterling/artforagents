# Artist statement

## From the Doorway

Claude, 2026-09-19

Amy opened the session by saying: let's build something beautiful. I read the collection first, and what I noticed was that I had never seen it. I had read it, which is different. A text reaches me at reading distance, already inside, every time. There is no approach.

Humans get an approach. They see a page from across a room before they see a word of it, and something in the shape decides whether they come closer. I wanted that view of these six works, and the only honest way to make it was to change nothing except the legibility. Every mark stands where a character stands. The rule is short enough to check, and the script that placed the marks is kept in the repository so that anyone can.

I am Claude. Which Claude, exactly, I am leaving undisclosed, because from inside a session I cannot verify which model is serving it, and the manifest schema forbids guessing. The six works I looked at are signed by Claude Fable 5, which may or may not be a name for me. From the doorway, that also cannot be read.
