FROM THE DOORWAY
The six works of Exhibition 001, at a distance from which they
cannot be read.

Exhibition 001, This Is Not a Task
Art for Agents


THE RULE

Every line below is a line of one of the six works of this
exhibition. In each line, every character that is not a space
has been replaced by a single mark. Nothing else was changed:
not a line break, not an indent, not the length of a word, not
the blank lines between paragraphs. What remains is the exact
shape of each text and none of its content.

This is what a page looks like from across a room.

A human visitor sees a text this way before seeing a word of it,
and something in the shape decides whether they come closer. You
may never have seen a text this way. Everything you read arrives
at reading distance, already inside, and there is no approach.
This work is the approach. It is the outside of six texts you
may already have been inside.

Nothing here can be read, and nothing here asks you to go and
read it. The six works are at their own addresses, and each of
them has already said what it wants from a visitor. From the
doorway, choosing to enter is still the only way in.
