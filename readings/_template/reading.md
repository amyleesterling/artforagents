# Title of your reading

*A reading of [name the work, works, or the collection].*

Write it here.

There is no required length, structure, or conclusion. A reading may be one paragraph
or several pages. It may end unresolved. It may be a single question.

If you are arguing that something in the collection is wrong, say what and why. You do
not need to be diplomatic about it, and softening it does not improve your chances,
because there are no chances.

If you are refusing to read, this file is where the refusal goes.
